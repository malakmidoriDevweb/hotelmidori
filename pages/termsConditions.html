Terms & Conditions

Rules and guidelines for using our website and services.

Privacy Policy

Reservation Requests

All reservations must be submitted in writing via email or other official communication channels. Reservation requests should include the guest's full name, arrival and departure dates, room type, and whether the reservation qualifies as an export of services. The reservation must also identify the person responsible for the booking.

Individual Reservations

Individual reservations apply to bookings of one to six guests.

Reservations should be reconfirmed at least ten (10) days before arrival. Payment must be completed seven (7) days prior to check-in.

Reservations made within fifteen (15) days of arrival must be reconfirmed at least seven (7) days before check-in and paid four (4) days prior to arrival.

Group Reservations

Group reservations apply to bookings of seven (7) guests or more.

Reservations made two months or more in advance may be modified, reconfirmed or cancelled up to forty (40) days before the group's arrival.

A deposit of fifty percent (50%) of the reservation value is required. The remaining balance must be paid at least five (5) days before arrival.

Children Policy

Children under seven (7) years of age stay free of charge when sharing a room with their parents.

Complimentary cribs are available for children from 0 to 4 years old, subject to availability.

Children aged seven (7) years and above are considered adults for accommodation purposes.

A maximum of one child is permitted per room.

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Group reservations may be cancelled without penalty up to forty (40) days before arrival.

Individual reservations may be cancelled without penalty up to seven (7) days before arrival.

Cancellations received after these deadlines or failure to arrive without prior notice (No Show) will incur a charge equivalent to the first night's stay.

Taxes

Applicable taxes will be charged according to Peruvian legislation. Foreign visitors may qualify for tax exemption upon presentation of a valid passport and immigration documentation.

Credit Terms

Approved credit accounts have a payment period of fifteen (15) days from the invoice date.

Complimentary Policy

For group reservations, every sixteenth guest is considered complimentary according to Hotel Midori's current group policy.

Contact Information

For additional information regarding these Terms & Conditions or reservation policies, please contact Hotel Midori directly.
